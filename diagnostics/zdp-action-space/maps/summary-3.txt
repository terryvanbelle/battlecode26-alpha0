## 14 KING_COMMS (81 options) kind: Mixed. There is 1 terminal 'accept zdb's writes' and 1 terminal 'undo all of zdb's writes'. Then a chained loop of up to 12 continue edits: 7 group undos, 3 BUDGET levels, 2 alert edits, 3 primitive actions (squeak, remove rat trap, remove cat trap) whose parameters come from KING_VAL digits, and 64 raw writes (the target is the slot index, the 10-bit value comes from 2 KING_VAL digits). The mean allowed count is 73.05 per record (measured), and 64 of those are raw SET options.
-- fallback_explanation: KING_COMMS never falls back, by construction. ZDB and DROP_ALL set D.lab=k (DK.java:827), and executed continue options set D.lab=k before D.emit (DK.java:833-834), so D.fallback (D.java:159-162: k>=0 && l!=k) is 0 for every record. An executor that returns false or throws drops the record instead (D.point=0, DK.java:832). That practically never happens: zdb's king never squeaks, RM is masked to removable tiles, and SET/DROP/band/alert always return true. Measured: fallback 0.0 for all 65 options picked in cpi-x0 (39,304 records), which matches the plan's 0% row (docs/rl-es-plan.md:501-510). The problem is the opposite of fallback. Every option acts, most edits hit zdb's protocol state with values the net cannot make valid, and the net is a constant function. widen's calibrated init (dpnet_io.py:296-304) gives a zero last layer, bias 0 on option 0 and -ln(10*81/0.005) = -12 on the other options. The resulting p_top is 0.9996, the measured deviation rate is 0.0051 (floor 0.005*72/73 plus a softmax leak of 0.0004), and the point is frozen in every RL config (trainable_points [5,7,10,11,12,13,16] in configs/rl-m2.json, cpi-x0.json, cpi-x1.json). Its floor therefore injects about 7.8 non-ZDB edits per team-game into every RL game, for both teams, with no credit: about 7.0 random raw slot writes, about 3.1 of them into MINE slots.
-- context_dependence: Strong, by design. The point edits zdb's turn. KC_ZDB means 'whatever zdb wrote this turn', DROP g means 'undo whatever zdb wrote in group g' (anything from a heartbeat to a formation opening), and SET i overwrites a protocol field. A field's role (coordinate, stamp, symmetry mask, budget, register) and the reach of a write (none, one round, permanent) depend on the slot and on state the features show only as raw values, such as whether a formation is open or whether MINE slot i is inside the list. Turn order matters: BUDGET and ALERT edits are undone by any later king's zdb writes in the same round (King.java:58-69, :83). The loop makes an option's meaning depend on the step (step 0 versus continuation). pending() treats the net's own band and alert edits as zdb's (DK.java:709; netSet is set only at :879).
-- features: 94 features (DK.java:724-758): loop step, kcEdits, kcSqueaked, pending-group bitmask (7 bits), pending popcount, coop, round/10, cheese/10, surplus/10+32768, rate100/10, numKings, King.slot, health, #enemies (cap 15), #cats (cap 4), #own babies in view (cap 63), actionReady, sawEnemy, own rat/cat trap counts in the 5x5, the heard-squeak block (6, D.java:681-684), the 64 raw shared-array values, and the changed mask as 4x16 bits.
-- missing_information: Missing: the king's own y (only decodable through its KING slot, indexed by the slot feature), map W/H, the exact round (round/10 only, so stamp freshness cannot be computed), the pre-turn values pre[] (what zdb's proposal replaced), the king's local state (Sym.mask, King.formSite/formStart, whether a formation is open), positions of sensed enemies/cats/mines (counts only), and whether another king acts later this round and will overwrite BUDGET/ALERT (King.java:58-69, :83).
-- generalization_proposal: Replace the raw editor with typed, protocol-valid comms. Keep zdb as the default and make exploration safe. (1) Keep KC_ZDB as option 0, so imitation labels stay valid. (2) Turn the DROPs into explicit per-group 'publish now / defer' choices that also update the king's local state: DROP FORM must roll back King.formSite/formStart (King.java:713-715), or be removed; heartbeat deferral should be a named 'hide' option, since 3 or more consecutive uses remove the king from readKings. (3) Fix pending() so all net edits count as net edits: add the band and ALERT_CALL/CLEAR slots to netSet (DK.java:847-856), so DROP_ALL and DROP g revert only zdb's writes. (4) Make BUDGET a team spend policy: a 3-level head (no traps or bites, traps only, both) plus 'zdb', written to a policy register that the babies' gates at Baby.java:802 and :850 combine with BUDGET. That way the King.java:83 writes of later kings cannot silently undo it. (5) Make ALERT a typed rally head over candidate targets: this king, each live king in Comms.kings, the last enemy-king sighting in R0-R2, or clear. Threat becomes an ordinal parameter, and there are two variants, one respecting zdb's same-round guard (King.java:63-64) and one overriding it. The executor always writes on-map x+1/y+1 and Comms.stamp(). (6) Make SQUEAK typed templates: SQ_MINE over candidate mines (sensed, or missing from the list), SQ_SYM carrying Sym.mask, SQ_CAT for a seen cat, SQ_EKING for a seen enemy king, plus one raw option whose type nibble is masked to net types (D.zdbType D.java:645), so raw exploration can never inject zdb-typed messages. Protocol slots are reachable only through typed executors that compute valid values. If raw access to every slot must stay for capability-completeness, keep it as one gated 'raw write' option with floor 0, whose value head sees the target's protocol role, me.x, me.y, W, H and round&1023. (9) Exploration: floor 0 at 14 and 15 until a typed head is trainable, and when exploring, spread the floor over option groups rather than over 81 options of which 64 are SET. ## 15 KING_VAL (32 options) kind: Parameters: one base-32 digit (5 bits) of a value, most significant digit first, chained over the prefix. The purpose feature (0 slot value, 1 squeak, 2/3 rat/cat trap tile) decides what the digits mean.
-- fallback_explanation: No fallback by construction: every digit record sets lab2=k (D.java:815), so D.fallback is 0, and a failure (no net, or a feature exception: dfail) aborts the parent KC option, which drops the KC record (DK.java:859, 868, 877 -> :832). Measured in cpi-x0: 425 sampled rows (about 16.6 per team-game in total), fallback 0, saturated 0.0047, per-value counts 6-23, consistent with uniform. The net is exactly uniform: VALUE_POINTS get all-zero biases and a zero last layer (dpnet_io.py:205, 302-304). It has no imitation labels, because KC_DECOMP is not implemented (docs/decision-points.md:213-214), and it is frozen in every RL config. Every value it produces is uniform noise.
-- context_dependence: Total. A digit's meaning depends on purpose, position, prefix and the protocol role of the target slot (x+1 coordinate, y+1 coordinate, 10-bit stamp, symmetry mask, budget/4, free register). Even with perfect training, the features cannot produce a valid location (no me.y, W or H) or a fresh stamp (no round&1023). Each digit is its own record sharing the game-level advantage, so credit for a specific 10-bit or 32-bit value has to be found by digit-by-digit exploration under bin-level advantages, which is hopeless in practice.
-- features: 22 features (DK.java:762-786): purpose, position, total, prefix high/low 16 bits, target slot idx (64 = none), current value of the target slot, whether the target is pending, trap mask low/high, coop, round/10, cheese/10, numKings, King.slot, me.x, and the heard block (6).
-- missing_information: Missing: me.y (KING_VAL has me.x but not me.y), map W/H (BABY_COMMS has them at D.java:630-633), round&1023 (needed for any valid stamp), Sym.mask, and positions of candidate targets (mines, cats, enemy king, own kings). There are no per-tile features for the RM digit (only the bitmask). The features do not state the protocol role of the target slot beyond its index.
-- generalization_proposal: Remove digit chains for protocol fields. Typed KING_COMMS options (see point 14) carry structured parameters. Location fields become candidate selection (own position, a live king, nearest enemy, nearest cat, a known or unlisted mine, the enemy-king guess), and the executor writes x+1/y+1. Stamps are always written by the executor as Comms.stamp(). Symmetry becomes a head over non-empty subsets of Sym.mask. Budget becomes an ordinal level. RM becomes a candidate head over own trap tiles with per-tile features. Keep base-32 digits only for raw payloads: register writes (R0-R2 and any freed slots) and net-type squeaks. Mask the squeak type nibble so it never equals a zdb type. Add me.y, W, H, round&1023 and the register's current value to the features. Keep floor 0 until the point is trainable, and when it is, initialise from structured defaults (for example, 'write my location') rather than uniform.
-- proposal_cost: Small. KING_VAL was never trained (uniform, frozen, unlabelled), so changing its option set or replacing it loses nothing. Cost: typed executors in DK.java (about 100 lines, counted within point 14's estimate), a few features in kingVal (DK.java:762-786), and dims in the point table, widen and build_dpnet.

-- cross_cutting:
   * For 14 and 15 the action space is under-structured, not over-constrained. 81 options, 73 allowed on average, and none ever fall back. But the few meaningful choices (publish or suppress, spend throttle, rally) sit beside 64 raw writes plus squeak and trap-removal digit chains whose values a constant or uniform net cannot make valid. Both points are frozen, so in every RL game they only add noise, about 7.8 non-ZDB king comms edits per team-game. The 0% fallback rows for KING_COMMS and the other pass-3 points in docs/rl-es-plan.md:501-510 come from how the code labels records (lab=k), not from executors acting usefully.
   * The fallback flag means different things at different points and is never used in training. D.fallback (D.java:159-162) flags label != pick, and BABY_MOVE uses label bit 0x8000 set in Baby.netMove (Baby.java:2193-2207). KING_COMMS instead drops the record when its executor fails (DK.java:832), and D.digits always sets lab2=k (D.java:815). So a failed KC pick disappears from the data (a selection bias that is about zero today), while at other points a failed pick is kept, flagged and trained on as if it had acted.
   * rl_data keeps the trailer's sampled option k, logq, mask and flags, never the executed label (rl_data.py:218-222; decisions.read_rl returns a label, scripts/decisions.py:97-100, but it is not stored). rl_train's ratio uses log p_theta(k) under the recorded mask, temperature and per-point floor (rl_train.py:380-384, 591), which equals the sampling probability: parity is checked at rl_data.py:236-253, and point 14 shows median 1.9e-5, p99.9 4.0e-5 over 39,304 records. For points with fallback, the gradient is therefore the correct REINFORCE gradient of the composite policy 'k, or zdb's cascade when k cannot act'. It is unbiased but costly. (a) The advantage of zdb's action is credited to k. (b) The nets never see whether an option can act, so they cannot learn to pick k only where it acts. (c) The flag only feeds summaries (rl_data.py:257-264, rl_train.py:536-542). Fix: evaluate the 'cannot act' predicates before sampling and mask those options, or record the executed option and train on the aliased action.
   * The entropy bonus and the KL leash are computed over masked option labels (rl_train.py:286-294, entropy_rows/kl_rows), not over behaviours. Aliased options add entropy with no behavioural diversity, and the anchor KL penalizes moves between behaviourally identical options. Aliased here means options that fall back, or that are behavioural no-ops such as ALERT_CLEAR on a stale alert or a BUDGET band another king overwrites the same round.
   * Rows are dropped silently when the recorded mask disallows k, and single-option rows are dropped too (rl_train.py:529-535); both appear only in metrics. KC has 0 single-option rows; KV has 2 (RM with one trap in range).
   * The exploration floor is spread uniformly over allowed options: floor/m each (DNet.sample DNet.java:116-135; per-point W.FL via DNet.floor DNet.java:31; default D.NET_FLOOR 0.02 at D.java:31). The per-option share therefore falls as the number of allowed options grows. On KING_COMMS (73 allowed) the 0.005 floor puts 0.0044 of probability per record on the 64 raw SET options and almost nothing on any one meaningful option. On frozen points the floor still runs in every game but is never trained, so it is uncredited noise in both teams' outcomes. Recommendations: floor 0 at frozen points, and floors spread by option group rather than by option.
   * Separate RNG streams: points 8, 9 and 14 and up sample from DNet.uniform2 (D.alt D.java:221; DNet.java:94-103), so switching comms exploration on or off does not shift other points' draws. This keeps exact replay intact; any restructure should keep it.
   * logq resolution: q = round(-ln p * 4096) is capped at 65535 (D.java:140-156), so it saturates for p < e^-16 (about 1.1e-7), and parity checks exclude saturated rows (rl_data.py:250). A typed head with very negative logits and floor 0 would record saturated logq for rare picks, which makes the importance ratio wrong. Keep a tiny floor or widen the q range.
   * The recorded mask is the post-fit mask (D.take clones DNet.lastMask, D.java:133-137; D.fit cuts options past W.A, D.java:227-240; DNet.reach DNet.java:25-28). Training therefore sees exactly the sampled support, staged unlocks included. A null mask is recorded as all-allowed (D.java:149).
   * Credit resolution: advantages are per team-game 10-round bins from a team-trace critic (rl_critic.py:255-268, GAE over bins, no per-decision state). Neither one-round comms effects (BUDGET band, ALERT rally) nor delayed persistent ones (SYM, MINE or FORM corruption) can be traced to the KC record that caused them. A small set of coherent typed options is a prerequisite for this point to learn anything.
   * Imitation side: the KING_COMMS label is always KC_ZDB (docs/decision-points.md:205). KING_VAL and BABY_VAL have no labels (KC_DECOMP not implemented, docs/decision-points.md:213-214). widen's calibrated init makes 14 a constant function and 15 uniform (dpnet_io.py:205, 226-229, 296-304). Training 14 failed at rl-m2 iteration 0 (KL 6 after one step; configs/rl-m2.json purpose). The cause was raw-scale hidden activations, which scripts/reinit_new_points.py:4-8 fixes, but 14 and 15 have stayed frozen ever since.

==================== map-hidden-strategy | keys: ['scope', 'strategy_summary', 'hidden', 'most_constraining', 'overrides', 'cross_cutting']

-- strategy_summary:
zdb, as replayed by zdp, puts the economy first, backstabs at a fixed early round and insures the king count at the end of the game.
1. Kings: each king settles on the nearest known home-half mine within dist2 400 of its start and never re-picks it (King.java:386, 927-943). Every turn it picks up all piles under its 3x3 (King.java:43, 179-188). It stays put whenever an enemy or cat is visible (King.java:384), and at war it bites the weakest adjacent enemy (King.java:57, 190-264).
2. Spawning: a king always builds a core of 18 babies (12 on maps of 441 tiles or fewer) while cheese covers cost plus a floor. Beyond that it builds only if the projected end-of-game surplus covers cost + 100; the projection uses 75% of measured income minus 200 per king (King.java:285-316; Econ.java:18-41). Babies are capped at 18 (40 with 1000 banked, 80 with 3000; King.java:267-271).
3. Roles at birth: the first 6 babies gather; later ones alternate gatherer and cat-fighter by round parity. Babies 4-15 born before round 300 are attackers that rush the mirrored enemy start. One in four gatherers scouts its own half until round 150 (Baby.java:318-342, 1189-1243, 1807-1809).
4. Gathering: gatherers spread over home-half mines, sorted by distance from home and offset by a per-rat slot (Baby.java:1473-1501). They wander the mine's 5x5 and chase visible piles. They deliver everything to the nearest king at 30 raw, at 10 when idle, and at any amount after round 1960 (Baby.java:1006, 1044, 1144-1157, 879-899). When a mine is crowded or idle they seek another (Baby.java:1830-1883).
5. War: from round 30 every baby is hostile, which ends cooperation (Baby.java:239-242; Cfg.java:146-148). Bites go to the king first, then the lowest-HP rat, with cheese added when that makes a kill. Babies grab weaker rats and throw them using a damage table (Baby.java:519-790). war() keeps gatherers defensive while fighters engage, and everyone answers a king's ALERT within dist2 225 (Baby.java:1245-1301).
6. Cats: gatherers flee within 20, fighters bite cats and defend kings, and cat traps go ahead of cats and, during cooperation, on cat lanes. From round 1850 at war every baby hunts cats for damage points (Baby.java:821-871, 1008-1016, 1405-1471).
7. King count: from round 80 (300 at war), the slot-0 king opens a second-king site once there are 16+ babies, 300 cheese and enough income (King.java:669-772). Two endgame paths (END_KING 1850, CASH_END 1880) aim to have 2 kings alive at round 2000, because kings weigh 0.5 of the war points (King.java:764-767; Baby.java:1579-1712; Cfg.java:75, 244).
8. Comms: kings own the 64-slot array (kings, symmetry, alerts, mines, cats, budget, formation), and babies squeak news to the kings (Comms.java:10-22; Baby.java:903-932; King.java:131-176).
9. Movement: greedy steps, then a bug walk, then reverse-A* flow fields, with automatic digging; macro steps strafe to face enemies in range (Nav.java:72-210; Flow.java).

-- cross_cutting:
   * Duplicate options dilute the uniform floor and split probability and credit among identical actions. KING_SPAWN has 5 identical holds among 22 options (DK.java:466-468, 477-478). The floor is spread uniformly over allowed options (D.java:30-31; DNet.java:30-31), so in cpi-x0 'stay' gets 8/33 of KING_MOVE's 0.1 floor.
   * Most trainable options are zdb's exit reasons (S_CAP, K_AT_HOME, F_CAP_OFF), not distinct actions, so each point has fewer real choices than options. KING_SPAWN: hold, dig the ring, or build at ring tile i. KING_MOVE: stay, flee or dodge, walk or dig toward home, walk to a pile, or step or dig in direction d. KING_FORM: close, hold, rich drop or gap, or open a site of some kind or ring offset. KING_ACT: no bite, a zdb-bite variant, traps, moat fill, dig the ring, or a cheese-boosted bite (DK.java:25-47).
